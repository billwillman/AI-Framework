	-Player Version vs Boss version

	changes. The differences are as following:
	.In Player mode, he won't gain Soul Spheres automatically, while in Boss mode he gains them without doing anything.
	.In Player mode his attack power is reduced.
	.In Player mode, he gains power half as fast as the AI version.
	.In Soviet Russia, Power gain YOU!!

    -USUAL KOF-TYPE GAMEPLAY MOVES-

	-POWER BUILD-
	A+B
	Sachiel gains power.You can gain up to 3 power stocks with this move.	

    	-DODGE-
    A+X or Z
    Sachiel dodges the opponent's attack.You can cancel a standing block into this if you
    have at least 1000 power.

    	-KNOCKDOWN ATTACK-
    B+Y or C
    Sachiel attempts to knockdown the opponent.You can cancel a standing block into this if you
    have at least 1000 power.

	-SPECIAL MOVES-
	
	-Soul Summon-
	HCB,P
	Sachiel summons a lost soul from the afterlife, which turns into a sphere that circles around him. Can be done
	up to 5 times.

	-Soul Lance-
	When you have 1 or more Soul Spheres
	HCF,K
	Sachiel uses the power of the Soul spheres to lunge at the opponent with a devastating kick. 
	LK version juggles, HK is multi-hit.
	
	-Soul Break-
	When you have 1 or more Soul Spheres
	HCF,P
	Sachiel commands the lost souls to hunt for the opponent and strike them. Homing projectile.
	
	-Seraph Sword-
	HCB,K
	Sachiel grabs his foe, and thrustes his angelic light sword into their skull.

	-Seraph Slash-
	F,D,DF,P
	Sachiel uses his angelic light sword to slash at the opponent in a semi-circular way.
   
	-DESPERATION MOVES- (NEED LV1 POWER)
	
	-Wandering Spirit
	When you have 1 or more Soul Spheres
	QCF,QCB+P
	Sachiel absorbs the power of the Soul spheres to dash towards the opponent, fully charged with energy, and
	deliver them a devastating uppercut.

	-Point of no return
	QCBx2+K
	Sachiel opens the gates of the Afterlife and commands the lost souls to hunt for his foe.
